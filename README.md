# Customer-insights-segmentation
The objective is to deliver a customer-focused analytical report that helps the company understand who their most valuable customers are and how to retain or target them better.

# Customer Insights and Segmentation 

## Project Overview
This project explores customer order data across geographical regions and customer segments to:

- Understand regional demand patterns and segment performance.
- Identify growth opportunities in underperforming regions & segments.
- Provide data‑driven recommendations to stakeholders.

## Objective
 
 - Analyze customer order data across regions and segments to identify sales trends.
 - Highlight high-performing areas.
 - Uncover growth opportunities.
 - Provide actionable insights to guide marketing and sales strategies.

## Tools used
- **SQL**
- **MySQL**: Data exploration and SQL queries
- **Power BI**: Visualizations
- **Notion**: Portfolio documentation and presentation

## Key Insights

- The Consumer segment drives most revenue: 46.9% of profit and 50.6% of sales.
- The top 10 customers contribute significantly to overall sales.
- High discounts in some regions/segments correlate with lower profit margins, indicating potential inefficiency.
- Data can inform seasonal trends and targeted monthly promotions.
- West and East lead in orders across all segments; South and Central lag, especially in Home Office and Corporate.
